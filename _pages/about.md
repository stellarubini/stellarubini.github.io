I'm a Data Scientist at Intesa Sanpaolo and a Visiting Scholar at the [Sky Computing Lab](https://sky.cs.berkeley.edu/), UC Berkeley.

My work focuses on **evaluating LLM-based and multi-agent systems**: designing evaluation pipelines that combine standard and custom metrics, to understand how these systems actually behave with real users and to turn that feedback into better products.

Alongside evaluation, I build Generative AI solutions end to end, from RAG chatbots and multi-agent architectures to document intelligence pipelines that combine OCR and LLMs, and I experiment with LLM fine-tuning. Before joining Intesa Sanpaolo, I spent three years as an Advanced Analytics Consultant at Avvale, leading AI projects in insurance, energy, and manufacturing.

I hold an MSc in Applied Mathematics from Sapienza University of Rome, and I've co-authored research on anomaly detection, from astrophysics to finance, and on Generative AI for technical reporting.
