I'm a Data Scientist at [Intesa Sanpaolo](https://www.intesasanpaolo.com/), currently a Visiting Scholar at the [Sky Computing Lab](https://sky.cs.berkeley.edu/), [UC Berkeley](https://www.berkeley.edu/).
{: .drop-cap}

I build Generative AI systems end to end, from RAG chatbots and multi-agent architectures to document intelligence pipelines that combine OCR and LLMs. I'm especially interested in the part that often comes last: measuring whether these systems actually work.

Before joining Intesa Sanpaolo, I spent three years as an Advanced Analytics Consultant at [Avvale](https://www.avvale.com/), leading AI projects in insurance, energy, and manufacturing. I hold an MSc in Applied Mathematics from Sapienza University of Rome.
